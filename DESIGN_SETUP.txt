🎨 SỬ DỤNG DESIGN MỚI:

1. COLORS - Sử dụng trong class:
   bg-primary          → Màu đen (#000000)
   bg-secondary        → Màu teal (#316763)
   bg-surface          → Màu nền (#fcf8fa)
   text-on-surface     → Màu chữ chính
   border-outline-variant → Màu viền

2. TYPOGRAPHY - Font classes:
   font-display        → Newsreader (cho tiêu đề)
   font-body           → Inter (cho nội dung)
   
   text-display-xl     → 64px headlines
   text-headline-lg    → 40px headlines
   text-headline-md    → 32px headlines
   text-body-lg        → 18px body
   text-body-md        → 16px body
   text-label-sm       → 14px labels

3. SPACING:
   max-w-reading       → 720px (optimal reading width)
   max-w-container     → 1280px (container width)
   py-section-gap      → 80px (section spacing)

4. COMPONENTS:
   <x-modern-card title="..." description="...">
       <x-slot:footer>
           <!-- Footer content -->
       </x-slot:footer>
   </x-modern-card>

==========================================
📝 VÍ DỤ SỬ DỤNG:

<!-- Tiêu đề với font Newsreader -->
<h1 class="font-display text-headline-lg text-on-surface">
    My Blog Title
</h1>

<!-- Nội dung với font Inter -->
<p class="font-body text-body-md text-on-surface-variant">
    This is body text with optimal readability.
</p>

<!-- Button với màu mới -->
<button class="px-6 py-3 bg-primary text-on-primary rounded-lg">
    Click Me
</button>

<!-- Card component -->
<x-modern-card 
    title="Article Title" 
    description="Article description here"
/>
